Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Workspace collects only what it needs to run your account and keep your projects. I’m Louis Tucker: I make and run Workspace, and I’m responsible for your data under UK data protection law.

What Workspace collects

  • Your account: your name, your email address and your password. Supabase, the sign-in provider, stores your password scrambled, so nobody can read it, including me.
  • Your projects: everything you put into Workspace, such as projects, tasks, dates, notes and the names of people you add to tasks.
  • Settings in your browser: Workspace keeps you signed in, keeps a copy of your projects so the app stays quick, and remembers light or dark mode. These are stored in your browser, not in cookies.

How I use it

I use your account and projects to run Workspace for you: to sign you in, save your work and show it on any device you sign in on. I only email you about your account, such as confirming it or resetting your password, and about important changes to Workspace.

Workspace doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and I never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores your account and projects and handles signing in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and app, and may keep short-term technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

They only handle your data to provide their service to Workspace.

How long I keep it

I keep your account and projects for as long as you have an account. If you ask me to delete your account, I remove your data.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of your data, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it. Email louistucker@live.co.uk and I’ll reply within a month.

If you’re not happy with my answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this page

If this page changes in a way that matters, I’ll tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
