Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Cronelo collects only what it needs to run your account and keep your projects. I’m Louis Tucker: I make and run Cronelo, and I’m responsible for your data under UK data protection law.

What Cronelo collects

  • Your account: your name, your email address and your password. Supabase, the sign-in provider, stores your password scrambled, so nobody can read it, including me.
  • Your projects: everything you put into Cronelo, such as projects, tasks, dates, notes and the names of people you add to tasks.
  • Settings in your browser: Cronelo keeps you signed in, keeps a copy of your projects so the app stays quick, and remembers light or dark mode. These are stored in your browser. The only cookie is one that tells this website you’re signed in to the app, so it can show “Open Cronelo” instead of “Log in”. It holds no personal information and is removed when you sign out.

How I use it

I use your account and projects to run Cronelo for you: to sign you in, save your work and show it on any device you sign in on. I only email you about your account, such as confirming it or resetting your password, and about important changes to Cronelo.

Cronelo doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and I never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores your account and projects and handles signing in.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and app, and may keep short-term technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service. It also runs the security check on the app’s sign-in screen, which tells people from bots.
  • Resend sends account emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.

They only handle your data to provide their service to Cronelo.

How long I keep it

I keep your account and projects for as long as you have an account. If you ask me to delete your account, I remove your data.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of your data, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it. Email hello@cronelo.co.uk and I’ll reply within a month.

If you’re not happy with my answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this page

If this page changes in a way that matters, I’ll tell you by email or in the app before the change takes effect.
